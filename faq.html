Do you need any help?

Here you can find the most asked questions that users search for. If you don't find the topic that you are finding, don't hesitate to contact us via our Email or Contact number, which can be found on the 'Contact Us' page.

How can I sign up to HopeForALL?

By following us on Twitter or Instagram and liking our Facebook page

By following us on Twitter or Instagram and liking our Facebook page, you can stay up to speed with all of the latest opportunities and information from the Our Volunteer Centre.

What happens if I feel I don't have enough time to volunteer?

It is common that you may not have enough time

In an effort to accommodate every taste, interest, and timetable, we make every effort to provide a wide variety of options. We hope to be able to assist you in finding something that would be of interest to you, whether you simply have a few hours to lend a hand with a one-time event or a free afternoon each week to go volunteer at a school.

What can I give a nonprofit?

what I an do on such conditon?

Even if you haven't previously volunteered, you already have a lot to offer and may not be aware of it. Numerous of our chances only call for a positive outlook and a little bit of time, but they're also a great method to develop your knowledge and experience in a field that fascinates you.

What should I consider before deciding to volunteer?

Depends on the opportunity you're interested in

Be sure to consider both what you would most enjoy doing To state the obvious, however, if you enjoy your volunteer experience, you are more likely to get more out of it! Be sure to consider both what you would most enjoy doing and what would be beneficial for you when deciding what project to participate in.

What should I do?

Really, the sky is the limit ...

Here are some examples to give you an idea of the variety of options: Volunteer at a significant charity (e.g. Red Cross, Amnesty International, Oxfam), Engage in local project work (e.g. a local committee, recycling centre, ...), Online volunteering (proofreading, translating, editing, CyberMentoring) and more.

What abilities are required?

Depends on the opportunity you're interested in

This actually depends on the opportunity you're interested in; certain jobs call for specialized knowledge, like knowing a certain language (for example if you are helping out a particular ethnic community). But don't let that stop you from venturing into uncharted territory; many organizations will train you in particular fields even if you have no prior experience, and you will undoubtedly pick up new abilities along the way!

Popular Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our mission and work.

How can I donate to HopeForAll?

You can donate through our website using the secure donation form. We accept both local and international donations.

Where does my donation go?

Your donation directly supports education, healthcare, food programs, and community development initiatives.

Can I volunteer with HopeForAll?

Yes! You can apply to volunteer through our website. We welcome individuals with diverse skills and backgrounds.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Depending on your country’s laws, donations may be tax-deductible. Please consult your local tax authority.

Are you ready to make a difference?

Join us in our mission to create a better future for all. Your support can change lives.

ABOUT US

Hope For All is a non-profit organization dedicated to transforming lives and building sustainable communities across Nepal. Since 2024, we've been working on the ground to provide education, healthcare, clean water, and economic opportunities to those who need it most.
